    fix(audio): rewrite playback engine to stream PCM, fix low volume, fix room echo causing false interrupts
    
    1. Replaced the buggy chunked WAV file concatenated source with a custom LivePcmStreamSource, completely eliminating 'audio stopped midway' and 'missed words' caused by Android ExoPlayer failing to load dozens of tiny WAVs.
    2. Added a 3.0x Software Gain directly on the PCM buffer inside LivePcmStreamSource because Gemini doesn't normalize baseline audio. This fixes 'voice output is still too low'.
    3. Reverted AVAudioSessionMode to voiceChat to re-enable the device's hardware Acoustic Echo Cancellation (AEC) – this allows voice volume to be handled safely by the OS without deafening the mic.
    4. Added a 1.2-second _echoIgnoreUntil mic grace period after the AI finishes speaking to stop natural room reverberations from bouncing back into the mic and falsely triggering Gemini's VAD.
