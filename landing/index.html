The Living Lens

A real-time, multimodal Live Agent that sees what you see, hears what you hear, and acts before you ask.

Explore Modes How it Works

Beyond Conversation

Arqivon doesn't just describe the world—it interacts with it through native Smart Action Cards and persistent memory.

Simultaneous Vision + Voice

Streams 2fps JPEG frames and 16kHz PCM audio concurrently over a single WebSocket for true multimodal understanding.

Zero-latency Interruptibility

Native Voice Activity Detection (VAD) means you can interrupt mid-sentence and the agent instantly re-focuses.

Agentic Tool Calling

13 registered tools bridge AI reasoning to native Flutter UI, rendering Smart Action Cards directly on your camera feed.

Four Specialized Modes

Switch contexts instantly. Each mode features dedicated system prompts, specialized tools, and custom UI overlays.

Assistant
