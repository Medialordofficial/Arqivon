Honest Audit

What's Holding Us Back

Our engineering is solid. Our surface layer is not. Here's what a premium product cannot afford to ship.

Color Identity Crisis

Home screen uses 4 different blues (#3B82F6, #0EA5E9, #818CF8, #22D3EE) with no clear hierarchy. All mode cards look the same at a glance. The Go Live gradient screams "SaaS template."

Zero Premium Signal

Generic AppBar titles ("SETTINGS", "ARCHIVE"). Default Roboto font. No visual metaphor for "Living Lens." The app looks like a developer prototype, not a product worth paying for.

Wasted Real Estate

40% of Home scroll space is a "How it works" tutorial section. A premium product is self-evident. No recent sessions, no personalization, no dynamic content to bring users back.

No Craft Details

No micro-interactions on cards. No entrance animations. Live screen audio mode is a blank white canvas. Settings has zero personality. Bottom nav is stock Material. Nothing says "we sweated every pixel."

Mode Cards Are Weak

120×120 circles with one icon and a label. No descriptions visible, no visual differentiation. The 2×2 grid wastes space and creates visual monotony. Users can't feel the power of each mode.

Live Screen Inconsistency

Mic button uses a purple gradient that doesn't match any brand token. Input mode toggle feels bolted on. Bottom area mixes text input, mic, and side controls in a layout that fights for space.

CONCEPT 01 / 06

Obsidian Lux

"Darkness as a canvas for intelligence."

A premium dark interface inspired by the world's most sophisticated tools. Deep blacks with carefully placed luminance — every element floats on darkness, making the AI orb the unmistakable hero. This is the design language of a product that costs nothing to try and everything to leave.

Arc BrowserLinearRaycastApple Intelligence

Design Principles

  • Dark surfaces with luminous accents — the orb glows, everything else recedes
  • Mode cards use subtle gradient tints — each has its own light signature
  • Go Live becomes a pulsing orb, not a rectangular button
  • Typography: SF Pro / Inter Extra Bold — sharp and modern
  • Premium depth through luminosity, not elevation or borders

CONCEPT 02 / 06

Warm Earth

"Intelligence that feels human."

Cream backgrounds, warm browns, organic shapes. This design says "we're not another cold tech tool — we're a warm, trusted companion." Serif headings and earth tones create intimacy. Users feel at home, not at a command line.

HeadspaceBear NotesAesopNotion (warm)

Design Principles

  • Cream (#FDF8F2) base with warm brown (#8B5E3C) as primary accent
  • Playfair Display serif for headings — editorial warmth, not cold tech
  • Hero section is a warm brown gradient banner with soft shadow
  • Mode cards use light tinted backgrounds with subtle borders
  • Every interaction feels like touching quality leather

CONCEPT 03 / 06

Glass Atelier

"Spatial depth meets crystalline clarity."

Inspired by Apple Vision Pro and iOS 18's layered glass aesthetic. A soft lavender gradient background with frosted glass cards floating above. Every element has depth through translucency. The result is three-dimensional and alive — like looking through a crafted lens.

Apple Vision ProiOS 18FigmaLoom

Design Principles

  • Lavender gradient base (#EAEDFF → #DFE3FF) — soft, dimensional
  • Frosted glass cards with white borders and real backdrop-filter blur
  • The orb is a purple-to-violet jewel — the "Living Lens" metaphor
  • Cards float as individual glass panels with micro-shadows
  • Depth through opacity layers and translucency, not drop shadows

CONCEPT 04 / 06

Editorial Noir

"Swiss precision meets editorial voice."

Black and white with one accent. Serif headlines. Hairline rules. Inspired by Monocle magazine and Dieter Rams. This design bets on typography and negative space — letting information architecture do the heavy lifting. Minimalism as the ultimate luxury.

MonocleThings 3BloombergDieter Rams

Design Principles

  • Near-white (#FAFAF8) with pure black text — maximum contrast
  • Playfair Display serif for all headings — immediate editorial authority
  • Modes as list items with color dots — zero fluff, high density
  • Go Live is a solid black pill — the most confident CTA possible
  • Archive entries are typographic, not card-based — newspaper index feel

CONCEPT 05 / 06

Aurora Vivid

"Bold color as a competitive weapon."

Dark base with vivid, saturated colors that pop. Each mode has its own unmistakable gradient identity. The hero section explodes with a full-spectrum gradient. The Live orb is a spinning conic gradient ring. This design screams confidence, energy, and "the future is here."

SpotifyDiscordVercelStripe

Design Principles

  • Deep dark base (#0C0C14) with vivid mode-specific gradients
  • Hero banner uses multi-stop gradient — indigo→pink→amber — unforgettable
  • Each mode card glows with its unique color through subtle background tints
  • The Live orb is a spinning conic gradient ring with dark center — mesmerizing
  • High saturation everywhere — demands attention, retains memory

CONCEPT 06 / 06

Zen Minimal

"The most powerful statement is silence."

Nearly monochrome. Maximum whitespace. Hairline details. This design strips away everything that doesn't serve the user. The Go Live button is a simple circle. Modes are a minimal list. So refined it feels invisible — and that's the point.

MujiPorsche DesigniA WriterBraun

Design Principles

  • Pure white (#FDFDFD) with minimal gray hierarchy — only 3 tones
  • Thin-weight typography: font-weight 300 for hero — luxury through restraint
  • Go Live is a circle outline — the most confident, understated CTA
  • Modes as clean list with hairline dividers — no cards, no boxes
  • Every pixel of whitespace is intentional — nothing competes for attention
